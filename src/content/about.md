I am a PhD student in Aerospace Engineering at [Princeton University](https://www.princeton.edu/) within the [Beeson Group](https://beeson.princeton.edu/people/), originally from Bologna, Italy. I graduated from the University of Oxford in August 2026 with a Master of Engineering (MEng). In my last undergraduate year, I was an exchange student in Princeton’s [Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE)](https://mae.princeton.edu/) department, where I worked on my [Senior Thesis](/files/Senior_Thesis_compressed.pdf) under the cross-departmental supervision of [Bartolomeo Stellato](https://stella.to) and [Ryne Beeson](https://mae.princeton.edu/people/faculty/beeson). My thesis was awarded the Franklin Prize for best Engineering Science Project at [Keble College](https://www.keble.ox.ac.uk/current-students/financial-information/awards-grants-and-prizes/). I have also collaborated with the [Oxford Control Group](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/control/) on two fully-funded research projects, under the supervision of [Kostas Margellos](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/kostas-margellos/) and [Idris Kempf](https://www.keble.ox.ac.uk/people/idris-kempf/).

My current research focus lies at the intersection of dynamical systems, optimal control, and astrodynamics. I am particularly interested in the application of numerical methods coupled with leveraging dynamical structures to identify and track bifurcations in low-fidelity astrodynamics systems such as the circular/elliptic restricted three-body problem (CR3BP/ER3BP), bicircular restricted four-body problem (BCR4BP), and Hill restricted four-body problem (HR4BP). I work on the `C++` development of [`DyLAN`](https://www.cuaerospace.com/spacecraft-solutions/mission-optimization-software/dylan), our in-house, [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/)- and [CUA](https://www.cuaerospace.com/)-licensed multibody trajectory optimization library, which has a (soon to be) public `Python` frontend, [`pydylan`](https://pydylan.github.io/). In addition, I am currently building [`TyCHO`](https://github.com/beeson-group/tycho), an open-source visualization toolkit for rendering interactive, three-dimensional astrodynamics runtime animations.

As an undergraduate, I co-founded the [Oxford Aerospace and Rocketry Society (OARS)](https://www.oxfordaerospaceandrocketry.co.uk/), Oxford's first rocketry team. I was also the 87th president of the [Oxford University Gliding Club (OUGC)](https://ougc.org/about_ougc/alumni_1937_club/), the oldest student aviation club in the UK. In my free time, I am an aspiring photographer and casual art enthusiast.
